## Hi there 👋
# Jehan | Computational Genomics & Precision Medicine 🧬

### Medical Genetics & Genomics | Bioinformatics | AI-Driven Precision Medicine

I am a Medical Genetics and Genomics postgraduate interested in applying computational approaches, bioinformatics and artificial intelligence to clinically relevant genomic research.

My research interests lie at the intersection of human genetics, computational biology, multi-omics and precision medicine, with an emphasis on translating genomic insights into clinical applications.

---

## 🔬 Research Interests

- **Computational Genomics:** Genomic data analysis, bioinformatics and multi-omics integration.
- **Rare Disease Genomics:** Variant prioritisation, pathogenicity assessment and genotype–phenotype correlation.
- **Reproductive Genetics:** Transcriptomics, implantation biology and molecular mechanisms of reproductive disorders.
- **Neurogenetics:** Genetic architecture of neurological and neurodegenerative disorders.
- **Computational Oncology:** Cancer genomics, gene-expression analysis and molecular classification.
- **AI in Precision Medicine:** Machine learning, predictive modelling and computational approaches to genetic diagnosis.

**Focus:** Reproductive genetics | RNA-seq | R | Differential expression
